# Revivea Sales Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive sales analytics dashboard designed to help management monitor revenue, orders, customers, profitability, sales channels, regional performance, product categories, and monthly sales targets.

**Created by:** Sathiyamoorthi S  
**Tool:** Microsoft Power BI  
**Reporting Period:** April 2026 – September 2026  
**Domain:** Sales Analytics | MIS Reporting

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to build an executive-level dashboard that consolidates key sales metrics into a single reporting view. It helps stakeholders monitor business performance, compare sales against targets, and explore trends across products, regions, and sales channels.

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Executive Overview
A consolidated view of revenue, orders, customers, average order value, profitability, and achievement.

### 2. Monthly Revenue Trend
Tracks monthly revenue from April 2026 to September 2026 to identify growth patterns and fluctuations.

### 3. Revenue by Channel
Compares revenue contribution from Marketplace, Organic, Meta Ads, Referral, and Instagram.

### 4. Top 5 Products by Revenue
Highlights the highest-revenue products to support product performance analysis.

### 5. Regional Performance
Compares sales across East, West, Central, North, North East, and South.

### 6. Product Category Analysis
Evaluates revenue across Innerwear, Shapewear, Loungewear, Activewear, and Accessories.

### 7. Actual vs Sales Target
Compares monthly revenue with sales targets to monitor achievement and identify performance gaps.

### 8. Interactive Filters
Allows users to filter the dashboard by date range, region, category, and channel.

## Business Questions Addressed

- How is monthly revenue changing over time?
- Which channels generate the most revenue?

- Which products and categories lead in revenue?
- How does actual revenue compare with monthly targets?
